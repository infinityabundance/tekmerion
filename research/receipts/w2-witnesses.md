## Development-only parts

The *payload* (accumulate set, congruence principle/term, flag meaning, bounds, case split, invariant) is derived. The following fragments of the close are still informed by the human proofs and are labelled `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY` in `tools/w4_close.py` and in the witness:

| key | what is transcribed |
|---|---|
| `post.accumulator_close` | DECARRY_RULE/DESUM_RULE then BOUNDER_TAC/REAL_INTEGER_TAC is the human arithmetic close. |
| `post.bitval_rewrites` | RULE_ASSUM_TAC(REWRITE_RULE[REAL_BITVAL_NOT; ADD_CLAUSES; VAL_WORD_BITVAL]) and the WORD_AND_MASK / WORD_UNMASK_64 sets are copied from the human closes. |
| `post.digit_expansion` | BIGNUM_LEXPAND_CONV / BIGNUM_EXPAND_CONV with the hand-picked LAND_CONV/RAND_CONV traversal is the human expansion idiom. |
| `post.side_condition_order` | The order of the congruence's side conditions (bounds first, case split last) follows the human proof; HOL does not force it. |
| `simulation.split_for_sub` | The sub routine is simulated in two carry chains with the borrow flag injected between them. The split point is derived from the flag's source step, but splitting at all follows the human proof. |
| `simulation.step9_cleanup` | RULE_ASSUM_TAC(REWRITE_RULE[WORD_UNMASK_64; NOT_LE]) after the CSETM step rewrites the flag-only step; copied from the human sub proof. |

In particular: the *order* in which the congruence side conditions are discharged, and the digit-expansion / accumulator-rewrite idiom that finishes them, are taken from the human closes. The side-condition *content* is witness-driven.
